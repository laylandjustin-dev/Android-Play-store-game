Pixel Town

Spend 10 points across five inherited traits. They are genetic to your people and apply for the whole run. Then fifty settlers drop onto a procedurally generated island and start living — working, eating, pairing, ageing and dying — alongside four rival civilisations running the same simulation. These ten points are yours alone: each rival picks its own traits to suit its character, so you never face four copies of your build.
